# Incident and recovery template

Status: no pilot incidents collected. An empty record means no evidence collected, not a claim of zero incidents.

Copy one record per incident:

- Incident ID, detection time, affected revision, participant ID: pending.
- Symptom and impact; affected workflow/version/run references: pending.
- Expected versus observed behavior: pending.
- Immediate containment and who authorized it: pending.
- Confirmed cause, supporting evidence, and unresolved hypotheses: pending.
- External effect checked; unsafe-action reconciliation decision: pending.
- Recovery steps, start/end times, backup timestamp, data loss or duplication: pending.
- Verification after recovery and authority to reopen admissions: pending.
- Follow-up issue, owner, due date, and review outcome: pending.

Never copy credential values, session cookies, tokens, payload bodies, or raw provider responses into this record. Link protected evidence by an approved reference. Use the [operations procedures](../implementation/phase-5-operations.md); restore into a new directory and retain source data. Escalate an uncertain outcome rather than assuming a retry is safe.
